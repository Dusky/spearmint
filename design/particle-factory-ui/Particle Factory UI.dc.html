Rules of the chrome
Four greys and two signal colours: amber for “the physics is telling you something”, green for “running”. Colour never decorates — if something is coloured, it changed. Numbers are monospace so readouts don't jitter while they tick. Labels are grotesk, sentence case, no shouting. Panels sit on one hairline and one background step; no shadows, no gradients, no textures.
